Recommendation

Option A best matches the design doc's philosophy: full-width cards maximize space for the filter distribution bar and session tables, the collapsible filter drawer keeps the feed clean by default, and it works well on tablets/mini-PCs (the target devices). The single-column layout also simplifies the accordion expand/collapse interaction.
