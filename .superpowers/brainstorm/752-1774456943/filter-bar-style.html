Single continuous bar with color segments. Percentages as inline labels or tooltip on hover. Compact, scannable.

Recommendation

Option A (Stacked Horizontal Bar) — matches the design doc's description of "a horizontal, color-coded stacked bar chart." The inline percentage labels are immediately scannable without needing a separate legend, and the bar visually communicates proportions at a glance. Works well at the card widths available in the sidebar layout.
